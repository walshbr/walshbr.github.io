The following co-authored posts refer to an ongoing writing project I am undertaking with Katina Rogers in which we mail a journal back and forth to each other over an extended period of time. In those  pages, we engage in an extended dialogue about writing, reading, and life. Posts collected here are a space to share excerpts as well as to reflect on the process as a whole.
