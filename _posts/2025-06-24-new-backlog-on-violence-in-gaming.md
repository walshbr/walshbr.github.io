I've got a new piece out this week for Grayson Morley's [Backlog](https://www.backlogmag.com/) newsletter, a semi-regular publication about the video games you haven't gotten to play yet. In it, I discuss the power of simple red pixels to stir more emotion than photorealistic violence ever could. Games discussed include _Arco_, _Drova: Forsaken Kin_, and (in passing) _Mortal Kombat_. Scott McCloud makes an appearance. It's always a treat to write about my other lifelong hobby, and I'm grateful to Grayson for giving me the space to do so. Check out "[On red pixels: violence and verisimilitude](https://www.backlogmag.com/violence-verisimilitude-videogames/)" over on Backlog.
