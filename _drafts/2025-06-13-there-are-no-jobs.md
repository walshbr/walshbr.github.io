Narrative about DH jobs is that they are plentiful, at least relative to other academic disciplines.
Been trying to help students find jobs
There have never been enough
Now there will VERY MUCH not be enough
A bubble has been burst, if there ever was one
Not just a matter of jobs but also opportunities for professional development training that otherwise might have helped students get jobs

What can we do? What can the DH community do to mitigate the damage? What is the role of those of us who offer professional development here? Have we become just like old academics who point to a job market that must inevitably recover but that will not?

What is our responsibility to the community? How do we change our plans? How do we be honest?

Things we can do

Make opportunities open and freely available
Prioritize students and early career scholars
Talk to students

Not meant to be a end point. Meant to offer questions
