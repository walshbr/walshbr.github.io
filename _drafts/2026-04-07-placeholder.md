I regularly spoke over the last year about blogging in terms of exercise. The more I blog, the easier it gets. Slowing down would only snowball. And sure enough, taking time off did exactly that. It feels hard to put words to the page now, more than ever. Writing regularly had become a kind of engine that I fed daily. I stopped fueling it, so it stalled out. 

In part, this was intentional. I just returned to work after two months of paternity leave. I'm eternally grateful to have had the time and space to focus on family. I know few are so lucky to have the time, though all deserve it. I knew that I would not write during that time. As a part of returning to work I'll be returning to the blog here as we adjust to the new normal, but it's hard to know exactly how to restart.
